# Prior Art and Intellectual Context

Invariant Thinking is intentionally positioned as a synthesis and operational framework, not as the invention of invariance or abstraction.

## 1. Mathematical invariance and structuralism

Mathematics has long studied properties preserved under transformations. Structuralist approaches likewise emphasize relations and structure rather than the intrinsic nature of particular representations.

Useful context:

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, **Structuralism in the Philosophy of Mathematics**: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/structuralism-mathematics/

ITF borrows the discipline that invariance is meaningful **with respect to transformations**, then applies that discipline to practical learning and technical comparison.

## 2. Analogy and structure mapping

Dedre Gentner's structure-mapping theory formalized analogy around relational structure rather than superficial object attributes.

- Gentner, D. (1983). *Structure-Mapping: A Theoretical Framework for Analogy*. Cognitive Science, 7(2), 155–170. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0702_3

ITF's **Link** stage is compatible with structural mapping, while **Test** makes failed transfer and analogy boundaries explicit parts of the workflow.

## 3. Transfer learning

Transfer learning studies reuse of knowledge across domains or tasks rather than learning each target entirely from scratch.

- Pan, S. J., & Yang, Q. (2010). *A Survey on Transfer Learning*. IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering, 22(10), 1345–1359. https://doi.org/10.1109/TKDE.2009.191

ITF is not a machine-learning algorithm. The relationship is conceptual: both care about what can be reused across changed contexts and when transfer fails.

## 4. Invariant representation learning

Machine-learning research studies representations that preserve task-relevant information while becoming invariant to selected nuisance variables. This literature is particularly relevant because it also exposes a warning: invariance can remove useful information.

- Moyer, D. et al. (2018). *Invariant Representations without Adversarial Training*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09458
- Zhao, H. et al. (2020). *Fundamental Limits and Tradeoffs in Invariant Representation Learning*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.10713

ITF adopts the analogous caution through the **Lossy Abstraction Principle**.

## 5. Schemas and reusable latent structure

Research in cognition and machine learning has explored schemas and latent structural templates as mechanisms for transfer and rapid adaptation.

One computational example:

- Guntupalli, J. S. et al. (2023). *Graph schemas as abstractions for transfer learning, inference, and planning*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07350

## What ITF proposes specifically

The proposed contribution is not any one ingredient above. It is the operational combination of:

1. the **Invariant Stack** for separating volatile manifestations from deeper structure;
2. mandatory **Transformation + Boundary** for invariant claims;
3. the **Learning Delta** as the residual target of deep study;
4. the **DELTA Protocol**;
5. explicit **analogy-break testing**;
6. **Invariant Maps** as shareable artifacts; and
7. a public RFC process for refining the framework through examples and counterexamples.

## Prior-art policy

If earlier work substantially overlaps any named ITF mechanism, open an issue or PR. The correct response is attribution and refinement, not defensive novelty claims.
